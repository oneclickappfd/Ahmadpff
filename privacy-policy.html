SC Vanish – Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SC Vanish (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. The App is published by [Your Name] (the "Developer"). Effective date: November 8, 2026. In short: SC Vanish works on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your photos or any personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID. The App does not show advertising, does not include third-party analytics or tracking, and does not send your photos or any personal data to our servers or to anyone else.

2. Photos and Media Access

The App asks for permission to access photos and media on your device (the Photos permission on Android 13 and newer, or the Storage permission on older versions). This access is used only to: • show your screenshots and camera photos inside the App; and • delete those photos when the time you selected in the Auto-Delete Schedule has passed. Your photos are read and displayed on your device only. They are never uploaded, copied or transmitted.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To work, the App stores a small amount of data locally in its private app storage: the deletion schedule you chose and the expiry time of each photo it tracks (identified by its location on your device). This data stays on your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Permissions We Use

• Photos / Storage: to display and delete the photos described above. • Notifications (Android 13+): to show the status of the background expiry check. • Background service: to keep checking when photos are due for deletion while the App is closed. • All files access (Android 11+, only if you choose to grant it): to delete expired photos without repeated confirmation prompts. It is used only for deleting the photos you scheduled. You can review or withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > SC Vanish > Permissions.

5. Automatic Deletion

When the time you selected is reached, the App deletes the matching photos from your device. Android may ask you to confirm the deletion. Deleted photos are removed from your device and cannot be recovered by us or by the App. You control the schedule and can turn Auto-Delete OFF at any time in Settings.

6. Third Parties

We do not share your information with third parties because we do not collect it. The App does not use third-party services that receive your data. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released. Google Play, the store where you download the App, may process information under its own privacy policy.

7. Data Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (such as screen lock and encryption). We do not operate servers that store your data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can at any time turn off Auto-Delete, revoke permissions in Android Settings, or uninstall the App. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California and have a privacy request, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available inside the App (Settings > Privacy Policy) and on the App's Google Play listing. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
